# Pavitra Panchang 365 — 90-Day Growth Execution Plan

Campaign window: 6 November 2026–4 February 2027  
Stretch target: 30 million Shorts views in 90 days. This is an ambitious target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## Publishing cadence

- Publish two original Shorts per day, subject to the quality gates below.
- 07:00 IST: daily Panchang, date-specific Tithi/Nakshatra, or a practical Muhurat highlight.
- 19:00 IST: a distinct explainer, vrat/festival context, Panchang concept, quiz, or next-day preview.
- Keep 40-second and 60-second versions as separate editorial experiments. Do not assume that duplicate uploads of the same script will perform equally; use distinct hooks and narration when testing duration.
- A weekly 4–8 minute explainer is an optional supporting format once the Shorts cadence is stable.

## Editorial mix

Across each 14-Short week:
- 5 daily Panchang explainers
- 3 Muhurat / timing explainers
- 3 vrat / festival explainers
- 2 Panchang knowledge explainers
- 1 Sanatan quiz or mythology story

Use a verified calendar and source material for festival observances. Do not invent a festival, vrat, muhurat, or regional practice to fill a slot.

## 90-day phases

### Phase 1 — Baseline and reliability (Days 1–14, 6–19 Nov)
- Stabilize the daily JSON → Hindi narration/TTS → FFmpeg → Remotion → artifact workflow.
- Validate the configured reference location and timezone before any public upload.
- Review both 40s and 60s renders on a phone-sized screen; keep the complete poster visible as designed and narrate only the most useful highlights.
- Test opening hooks and track the 24-hour and 7-day results by duration and content pillar.
- Establish the initial performance baseline; do not scale volume to compensate for weak retention.

### Phase 2 — Format validation (Days 15–30, 20 Nov–5 Dec)
- Compare hook styles, subject pillars, and 40s vs 60s performance.
- Improve the first 1–2 seconds using actual audience-retention evidence.
- Build repeatable but materially distinct formats for timing explainers, festival context, and Panchang education.
- Start one weekly long-form explainer if production quality remains stable.

### Phase 3 — Scale proven formats (Days 31–60, 6 Dec–4 Jan)
- Prioritize topics and formats that outperform the channel baseline.
- Batch research and production while preserving date-specific factual validation.
- Consider a third daily Short only if retention, originality, and production QA remain healthy.
- Use viewer questions and comments as inputs for new explainers.

### Phase 4 — Concentrate and optimize (Days 61–90, 5 Jan–4 Feb)
- Concentrate on the strongest content pillars and hook formats.
- Publish sequels to proven topics without reusing the same script or merely swapping dates.
- Align festival and observance coverage to a verified calendar.
- Review weekly results and prepare the next 30-day editorial plan.

## Measurement

Record each upload with:
- Video ID, publish date/time, topic, content pillar, hook type, duration, and TTS provider
- Views and engaged views
- Stayed-to-watch / chose-to-view
- Average view duration and average percentage viewed
- Subscribers gained, shares, comments, and likes
- 24-hour, 7-day, and 28-day results

Review weekly:
1. Rank uploads by 7-day views and engaged views.
2. Compare stayed-to-watch and average percentage viewed by duration.
3. Compare subscribers gained per 1,000 engaged views.
4. Identify topic/hook patterns among the top and bottom performers.
5. Write follow-up topics based on evidence; avoid changing multiple variables at once.

## Internal improvement targets

These are working targets for iteration, not YouTube eligibility thresholds:
- Improve stayed-to-watch from the historical 25.5% baseline toward 40%, then test for 50%+.
- Improve average percentage viewed from the historical 60.4% best-Short baseline toward 70%, then test for 85%+.
- Evaluate average view duration in relation to video length.
- Track subscribers gained per 1,000 engaged views, plus shares and comments.

Historical baselines are dated snapshots and must be replaced with current YouTube Studio exports when available.

## Quality gates before every public upload

- Date, weekday, Tithi, Nakshatra, and other Panchang values are generated from one validated JSON payload.
- Reference coordinates and timezone are explicit and visible in the video's timing disclaimer.
- Festival/vrat observance and regional details are checked against reliable sources.
- Hindi narration matches the poster data; pronunciation, duration, and audio levels are reviewed.
- Devanagari text is legible on a phone; no clipping, blank frames, missing assets, or unintended silence.
- Titles and descriptions are accurate and not keyword-stuffed.
- Each upload adds distinct informational or editorial value. A repeated visual identity is acceptable; mass-produced, interchangeable videos are not a sound monetization strategy.

## Monetization and reporting

Treat 30M views as the growth stretch goal, not as a promise of monetization. Monitor the YouTube Studio Earn tab for the channel's current eligibility and distinguish total Shorts views from eligible/engaged views used in monetization calculations. Preserve source notes and calculation inputs for factual review.
